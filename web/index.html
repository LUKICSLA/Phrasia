What's inside

Passphrase generator

  • Slovak, Czech and English word lists – or your own
  • Mix languages, separators, numbers and capitals
  • Strength and “time to crack” for every result
  • Presets and several variants at once

Password vault

  • Title, username, password, URL and notes
  • Health report: reused, weak and old passwords
  • Clipboard that clears itself, auto-lock
  • Backups and header backup / restore

Private by design

  • Works completely offline
  • No account, no sync, no telemetry
  • Optional screen-capture protection
  • Light and dark theme, follows Windows

Security in short

Argon2idkey derivation, tuned to your PC
AES-256-GCM + ChaCha20two layers of authenticated encryption
PIMa secret number on top of the master password
Device-boundvault is tied to your PC and Windows account

The vault is only as strong as your master password and PIM. Not sure? Let Phrasia suggest one.

Good to know

Export your device key

Right after creating a vault, use Export device key… and store the file safely. Without it you can't open the vault on another PC or after reinstalling Windows.

No password reset

Forget the master password or PIM and the vault is gone. There's no back door – that's the point.

Not audited

Phrasia is a personal project without an independent security audit. Keep backups and use it on a computer you trust.
